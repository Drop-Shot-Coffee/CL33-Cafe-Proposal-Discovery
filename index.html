How this works

Nineteen short answers, from whoever holds each file.

This form pairs with Appendix A of the structural proposal. Each answer sizes a specific part of the deal — the works, the term, the rate, the opening date — and none of it commits CenterLine to anything. Answer in pieces, in any order, no single sitting needed: everything saves on its own as you type, and you can close the page and come back whenever suits.

— of these are rate inputs. They are the answers the revenue-share numbers are computed from — Drop Shot returns the completed rate proposal within ten days of receiving them. The rest sequence the works and the paperwork, and can follow at their own pace.
Using the form. Type straight into a field, or mark it Not applicable or We’ll follow up if the answer needs digging out — a marked field is a real answer and we will simply pick it up together. Every field carries its own formatting toolbar — styles, lists, alignment, colour, links, undo. Tab indents, Shift+Tab outdents; press Escape to step out of a field. Pasted text arrives clean. Everything autosaves about a second after you stop typing, and the Save all button is there for reassurance.
Questions? Anything unclear, write it into the nearest field or send it straight to Tarek at hello@dropshotcoffee.us — the form is a convenience, never a gate.
